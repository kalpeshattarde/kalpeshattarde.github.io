02 / System thinking

From trigger to measurable outcome.

A typical system connects the business event to automation logic, data, communication and reporting — with logs and failure handling around the workflow.

Business Event

Form, payment, message, schedule or CRM change.

→
Automation Layer

n8n, Pabbly, Apps Script, webhooks and APIs.

→
Data + AI

Sheets, SQL, classification, summaries and routing.

Design principle: automate repeatable work, keep humans in the loop for exceptions, and document how the system is operated.
