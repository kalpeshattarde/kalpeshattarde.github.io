## Business Impact Framework

I design automation around three practical outcomes:

1. **Time saved:** Remove repetitive entry, screening, routing, reminders, reporting, and coordination.
2. **Revenue capacity created:** Move qualified leads faster, reduce missed follow-ups, support retention, and free people for higher-value work.
3. **Operational visibility:** Convert scattered events into structured records, dashboards, alerts, logs, and next actions.

These are intended business mechanisms. Specific revenue increases, hours saved, and ROI should be added only when measured and verified for the relevant implementation.

## Delivery Approach

1. Understand the business model, operating process, and growth objective.
2. Identify bottlenecks, manual work, data gaps, and system dependencies.
3. Design workflow logic, data boundaries, approvals, and integrations.
4. Build the smallest useful version with validation and error handling.
5. Test normal cases, edge cases, duplicates, failures, permissions, and provider limits.
6. Add monitoring, reporting, documentation, and operational handover.
7. Improve the system using actual operating feedback.
